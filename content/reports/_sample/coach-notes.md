What a season. Twenty-three matches, a runner-up finish in the league and a cup semi-final: this group should be proud of every minute.

When we started in September, most of you had not played together before. By Christmas you were passing like a team that had been together for years. That didn't happen by accident. It happened because you turned up to training in the cold, listened, and kept trying things that didn't always work first time.

## What I will remember

- The **6–0 win** against Brook Green, where every single player got a touch in the box.
- Isaac's goals, and the fact that he would tell you the assist was the best bit.
- Alfie's save in the Kingsford cup tie that kept us in the game.
- Finn organising the midfield at the age of nine, and nobody being annoyed about it.

> Winning is great. Playing the right way and looking out for each other is better.

## To the parents and guardians

Thank you for the lifts, the oranges, the early mornings and the patience on the touchline. You are a big part of why the boys enjoy this.

## Looking back

The league was decided by a single point, and we know where we dropped them: away at Marsh Lane in September and at home to Kingsford in February. We learned from both.

Our best football came after the winter break. We scored **thirty-one goals in nine matches** and conceded only seven.

Keep enjoying it. See you in pre-season.
